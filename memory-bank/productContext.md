# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
Healthcare accessibility is a major challenge. Patients often struggle to find the right doctor, book appointments, and get timely consultations. This platform bridges the gap between patients and healthcare providers through technology.

## Problems It Solves
1. **Difficulty finding doctors** - Centralized search by category, name, and specialization
2. **Appointment management** - Streamlined booking with time slot selection and serial numbers
3. **Remote consultations** - Chat and video calls eliminate geographical barriers
4. **Prescription management** - Digital prescriptions reduce paper waste and improve access
5. **Medicine access** - Direct ordering from prescriptions simplifies the process
6. **Trust & transparency** - Rating/review system helps patients choose quality doctors

## How It Should Work
1. **Registration** - Users sign up as Patient, Doctor, or Admin
2. **Doctor Search** - Patients browse/search doctors by category or name
3. **Booking** - Select a doctor, pick a time slot, receive appointment number
4. **Consultation** - Chat or video call at appointment time
5. **Prescription** - Doctor writes digital prescription after consultation
6. **Medicine Order** - Patient orders medicines directly from prescription
7. **Payment** - Secure online payment for consultations and medicines
8. **Follow-up** - Rate doctor, save to favorites, rebook easily

## User Experience Goals
- **Simplicity** - Max 2-3 steps for key actions
- **Speed** - Fast loading, real-time updates
- **Trust** - Professional healthcare design, clear CTAs
- **Accessibility** - Mobile-first, high contrast, readable fonts
- **Delight** - Smooth animations, intuitive interactions
